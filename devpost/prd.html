Freelancer Swipe

Small clients swipe through freelancers for a skill, save the ones they like, and instantly get their contact info, plus a quick form for freelancers to join the deck.

From scope to product

Scope said "client enters a skill and swipes". Now we know exactly what that means: a Tinder-style role screen, skill chips with a type-your-own fallback, one big best-project image per card, swipe or ✕/✓ with a back button, a save that reveals contact instantly, and a Saved list that survives a restart. Scope also gained one thing: a minimal freelancer form, so the demo closes the loop on both sides.

The kernel. One card at a time, judged in seconds by one image.

  • One large best-project image, name, location, skills.
  • YouTube/TikTok link visible; tap opens the video.
  • Swipe left / ✕ skips · swipe right / ✓ saves.
  • ↶ returns to the previously skipped freelancer.
  • After the last card, the deck restarts from the first.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: no waiting, no proposal.

  • Saving shows that freelancer's contact info immediately.
  • The freelancer is added to the Saved list.
  • Every saved freelancer with name, image, contact info.
  • Still there after closing and reopening the app on the same device.

Assumption: empty list shows a short "nothing saved yet" message.

Look and Feel

From the learner's Tinder reference: orange-to-pink gradient, bold white headline type, big white pill buttons, mobile-first. On the card, the project image dominates and text is secondary.

Boundary

Building now
  • Role screen · minimal freelancer form · skill picker · swipe deck (swipe + buttons + back)
  • Save → contact reveal · Saved list that survives restart
  • Sample freelancers · no-match notification · deck loop
Deferred
  • Accounts/login: Saved list is per device instead
  • Editing/deleting a freelancer profile
  • Image upload: pasted link instead
  • Shared server database (SQL Server): planned for later
Non-goals
  • In-app messaging, calls, scheduling: the platform only connects people
  • Job posting and proposals: the slow flow this replaces
  • Multiple images per card: one best image, by design
