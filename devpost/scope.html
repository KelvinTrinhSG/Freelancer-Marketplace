Freelancer Swipe (working name)

A Tinder-style app where clients swipe through freelancers for a skill they need, save the ones they like, and get their contact info right away.

The Unique Kernel

Flip the hiring flow: instead of posting a job and waiting for proposals, the client actively browses freelancers one card at a time and decides in seconds based on portfolio images of past projects. A save instantly reveals how to contact them. The platform only connects people; the conversation happens elsewhere.

Old flow vs. this flow

Who It's For

Individual clients and small, low-budget startups with short, specific, fast jobs: a short video, testing a game. They want to pick someone they like and talk to them now, not post-and-wait on Upwork or Facebook groups.

Why This Matters

"Khách hàng tìm Freelancer rất nhanh."

Clients find freelancers very fast. Learner's course goal: learning how to work with an agent.

The Core Loop: step through it

They come back whenever a new small job comes up. Feel: Tinder-like, fast, light, visual-first; portfolio images dominate the card.

What "Working" Looks Like

A client opens the app, enters a skill, and the app pulls matching freelancers from the database and shows them one by one. The client swipes through and saves a few.

"Oh, that's cool" beat: a save immediately shows that freelancer's contact details, and the saved freelancers are waiting in a saved list.

Boundary

Now: the proof of concept
  • Skill search → matching freelancers from a database
  • Swipe card UI, one freelancer at a time: name, location, skills, portfolio/project images
  • Swipe past or save
  • Save reveals contact info + contact methods, adds to a saved list
  • 10–20 pre-made sample freelancer profiles with portfolio images (seed data)
Later: worth doing, not now
  • Freelancer self-registration: profile, portfolio, skills, project images, self-promotion
  • Enforcing that freelancers must have portfolio, skills, and images before appearing
  • Client accounts/login so saved contacts persist per client
Cut: with reasons
  • In-app messaging, calls, interview scheduling: the platform only connects people; contact happens elsewhere
  • Job posting and proposals: exactly the slow flow this idea replaces
